Mission Statement

We all deserve to flourish through purposeful, intrinsically motivating work but too often, the invisible systems and day-to-day operations beneath the surface quietly work against us. At Understory Consulting, I help mission-driven leaders eliminate operational friction and connect everyday work to lasting impact.

Read my theory of change to understand the logic behind my work, or see how I translate it into hands-on partnership with your team.

My Background

My work is guided by a simple, human principle: leave it better than you found it.

I bring this mindset into organizations, partnering with leaders to transform complex operational challenges into sustainable solutions that align their people, processes, and technology. My approach integrates a decade of hands-on expertise in tech organizations across industries with the scientific rigor of a Masters in Management Science & Engineering from Stanford. My sweet spot is organizational development, leadership development, purposeful work, and play-based learning.

The outcome of our work together won't just be a plan-it will be lasting alignment that gives your teams the clarity, capacity, and momentum they need to truly thrive. You made the right decisions to get your team here, now I can help you get where you're going next.

This same principle drives my public work as a LeadDev speaker, podcast host, and content creator. I turn hard-won lessons into accessible resources for other practitioners and leaders because sustainable change happens when wisdom is shared, not hoarded.

The real reward is seeing those ideas take root as others gain clarity and tools to build more effective, human-centered organizations that they, in turn, can leave better than they found.
